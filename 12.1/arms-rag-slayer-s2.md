**Authority:** Wowhead (Artiros + APL team) is the simplified, up-to-date source. This guide is niche overlay and Discord Q&A. Do not encode minute optimizations that break GCD flow.

## Talents (keep 2–3 pages, not 15)

ST vs 2T vs 3T+ gaps are small. Alter on the fly.

- **ST:** Fierce Follow-Through over Opportunist (gap is ~zero; skip tracking Opportunist). Relentless Pursuit over Vicious Agility in raid (charge move speed). Second Wind if needed; can drop Intervene/Interpose.
- **Raid multi:** Broad Strokes, Dreadnaught; Powerful Momentum for 2T cleave. Some bosses (adds / intermission): drop Bloodsurge for Fervor of Battle if several Cleaves can funnel.
- **M+:** Fervor of Battle + Collateral Damage for mass AoE. Fatality is the raid pick (tiny DPS gap, much more forgiving).

### Fervor of Battle

Cleave hitting 3+ also Slams the primary. That Slam can proc Heroic Strike. **Never Heroic Strike in AoE** — you lose the extra HS damage and a GCD.

### Collateral Damage

Raid: Fatality, ignore Collateral unless a huge add phase. M+: more prio damage. **2T M+:** Cleave at **3 Collateral stacks**, then Mortal Strike (Cleave edges MS there). Spending Collateral is not a separate #1 button — you Cleave, so you spend it.

### Executioner's Precision (EP)

At **2 stacks, always Mortal Strike** (~70% more). Never MS without 2 EP in execute. Bladestorm (Unhinged) casts MS and **consumes EP** — if you enter BS with 2 EP, you need two Executes after BS before the next MS.

**AoE: do not care about EP stacks.**

### Tactician

Juggle Execute, Overpower, Cleave, (ST) MS so you **Slam as little as possible**. Wowhead order of mind: Cleave as much as possible → dump Sudden Death so you are not at 2 (overcap) → Overpower so you are not at 2 OP stacks.

### Dreadnaught

Procs off Sweeping Strikes Overpower. Does **not** rewrite the APL so much as **positioning**: the line comes out of the mob behind it. Stand so the line hits as many as possible, and Cleave that line. This is why Overpower is strong in M+ AoE.

### Sudden Death (SD)

2 stacks. Next Execute: free, any HP, damages as 40 rage. Feeds Anger Management and Slayer procs. Do not overcap.

### Imminent Demise + Unhinged

**ST opener / first BS:** Execute **before** Bladestorm so you have ≥1 ID stack → extra Unhinged MS inside BS. Later BS almost always already have 3 ID from SD procs.

**AoE:** that extra MS is weak. Execute **after** BS for **Bladestorm CDR**. More BS = more AoE.

### Tactical Edge

1) Instant SD for ID right before ST Bladestorm. 2) Line BS with Colossus Smash: if you have Executioner stacks and Smash is ready but BS is ~20s, Smash → Execute (guaranteed SD) to pull BS in.

### Unrelenting Onslaught (Executioner)

Stacks to 5. Each stack −5s BS CD. Stacks are not a clean 5→0; they wave independently. Average player: **ignore for APL**, press Wowhead. Niche: fish procs at end of pack for next-pack BS, or to line Smash. Fresh ST pull has **zero** Executioner — first Tactical Edge SD will not CDR BS yet, so the opener ID Execute is still correct (damage now, CDR later).

### Show No Mercy

Execute hits harder with more Executioner stacks. Niche: execute-phase small pack, **4+ stacks**, Execute on CD (rage dump + AM CDR). Not default APL.

### Anger Management

Execute (up to 40 rage) CDR Smash, Bladestorm, Avatar. End of pack can turn 45s CDs into ~15–20s. Niche after fundamentals. Default: send CDs if unsure.

### Opportunist

2 stacks can beat a nathry Execute / single SD. **Do not track in APL.** Mental cost > gain. Skipping is viable.

### Stance Mastery (Defensive Stance)

~30% DR on a 3s CD, effectively always up. Top-tier with AMS / Spell Reflect. Deaths that are not avoidable damage are usually missing D-stance / potion / Impending Victory.

### Master of Warfare + 4pc

Already covered by existing Cleave (AoE HS via FoB) and ST HS > MS. **Do not track MoW. Do not play around 4pc.** Rotating around 4pc is tiny gain or a loss.

### Bloodletting / Rend

Rend on a target = +5% crit (Bloodletting). **Cleave on opener before anything else** so Rend lasts the whole Smash window. Apply on pandemic. Refresh if Smash is about to come and Rend has **<10s left** so full duration covers CDs. This season it matters even in ST. **ST execute:** MS applies Rend — stop worrying. **AoE:** still maintain Rend (nameplates / tracker).

### Reap the Storm

Cleave on 3+ has 20% chance of a free AoE flurry and applies Overwhelmed. Why Cleave is mandatory even without Collateral. **Every ~3rd GCD in AoE should be Cleave.**

## Flow over micro-opt

Do not delay Bladestorm to stack EP. Do not stall GCDs to spend Opportunist. Wrong GCD / delayed BS loses more than the “optimal” press. Slayer Arms is **flow**: Rend opener → ~14–15s Rend again → ~10s later CDs → Rend again before CDs. Dummy practice beats encounter-specific exception hunting.

---

## Openers

**ST:** Charge (rage) → Cleave (Rend) → Avatar + Colossus Smash + unused trinket → Tactical Edge SD **Execute** → Bladestorm (extra Unhinged MS) → optional Storm Bolt during BS → HS if it procced → then MS / OP. Hold a **single** SD gained during BS briefly to build Executioner for BS CDR; **2 SD dumps** (overcap).

**AoE:** Precast Sweeping Strikes (10/15/20s mark) → Charge → Cleave (Rend + damage) → Avatar + Smash → **Bladestorm with no pre-Execute** (want BS CDR). Cleaves generate more Slayer strikes than MS (MS does not Sweep-cleave).

**2T:** ST rotation + maximize Sweep uptime. Precast Sweep. Recast Sweep at **≤6 stacks** (7–8 wastes the 12 you gain; 4–5 wastes CD tick). Two-target = ST with Sweeps. Multi = Cleave on GCD.

**M+ boss after packs:** you usually already have 3 ID — treat like AoE (hold Execute for BS CDR, do not ST-opener Execute). First pack: optional SD fishing **on gather only**; once stacked, send the pack opener. Do not fish after pull is together.

## Rotation gist

- **AoE:** Always be Cleaving. Cleave every 3 GCDs. Wowhead: Cleave (even without 3 Collateral) sits just under 2 SD. Those 2 SD are **overcap**, not because they beat Cleave. Play for CDR and Cleaves. Never Slam.
- **ST:** Same shape with Mortal Strike. Execute phase: replace MS with **2 Execute → MS**.
- Press highest Wowhead priority as often as possible. CDs are frequent — send them.
- **ST Sweeping Strikes:** no.
- **Bladestorm:** ST with Smash. AoE: send when available; if Smash has **≥10s remaining**, sending BS is fine (“lazy”).
- **Execute vs Overpower (no SD):** Execute over OP unless no rage (Wowhead).

## Positioning / hitboxes

Cleave cone and Dreadnaught lines are finicky and high value. Overpower during Sweep can send two Dreadnaught lines in different directions. Stand slightly into the pack so Cleave hits 3+ (FoB). Dreadnaught from a front mob can miss the pack — step so the line goes through the clump. Slayer Strike spins around **you** — closer = more hits. Same for Bladestorm radius. Same buttons, worse position = far less damage (cleave hits, OP damage). Smash/BS are uptime CDs (weaker than e.g. Shaman Ascendance) — you have to deal damage **all the time**.

Existing LPARM Smash-gather gate is the APL stand-in for this. Do not delete it.
