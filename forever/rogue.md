# Rogue — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Rogue guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/assassination-rogue/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/combat-rogue/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/subtlety-rogue/
- https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/rogue-leveling-guide

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

**Poisons (all specs):** Instant Poison on the main hand, Deadly Poison on the off hand.

## Assassination

**Priority:**
1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
2. Shank while below 70 energy.
3. Cold Blood, then Eviscerate at 5 combo points.
4. Eviscerate when it won't delay Slice and Dice.
5. Garrote from Stealth.
6. Mutilate as the builder.

**Build note:** takes the Venom talent.

## Combat

**Priority:**
1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
2. Blade Flurry, Adrenaline Rush and on-use effects together.
3. Shank while below 55 energy.
4. Eviscerate when it won't delay Slice and Dice or Rupture.
5. Garrote from Stealth.
6. Sinister Strike.

## Subtlety

**Priority:**
1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
2. Garrote.
3. Rupture at 5 combo points.
4. Keep the Hemorrhage debuff up.
5. Death Mark at 5 combo points (pool energy for it).
6. Premeditation below 4 combo points.
7. Shank below 4 combo points at 70 energy.
8. Ghostly Strike (it lets Rupture and Garrote be used outside Stealth).
9. Hemorrhage as the builder.

## Leveling

Source: Maxroll's Rogue leveling guide.

- **Rotation:** open with Ambush, Riposte when it procs, Shank without capping energy, Sinister Strike, keep Slice and Dice up, Expose Armor on tough mobs, Eviscerate at 5 combo points (or 3+ if the mob is nearly dead).
- **Poisons:** available from level 20.
