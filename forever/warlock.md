# Warlock — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Warlock guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them. No leveling guide was found for Warlock.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/affliction-warlock/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/demonology-warlock/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/destruction-warlock/

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

## Affliction

**Priority:**
1. Spellstone.
2. Demonic Sacrifice the Succubus.
3. Your assigned curse, otherwise Curse of Shadow.
4. Shadow Bolt on Nightfall procs.
5. Bane of Doom if the target will live longer than a minute, otherwise Bane of Agony with Amplify Curse.
6. Corruption.
7. Siphon Life.
8. Wrack as the filler (level 30 talent, replaces Shadow Bolt).
9. Life Tap while moving.

## Demonology

**Priority:**
1. Firestone.
2. Demonic Sacrifice the Succubus.
3. Summon the Imp.
4. Soul Link.
5. Your assigned curse, otherwise Curse of the Elements.
6. Bane of Doom or Bane of Agony.
7. Immolate.
8. Corruption.
9. Soul Fire below 35% health with Decimation.
10. Searing Pain as the filler.
11. Life Tap.

**Notes:** Demonic Pact lets you keep a demon out while sacrificing another. Takes Demonic Brand.

## Destruction

**Priority:**
1. Firestone.
2. Succubus.
3. Curse of the Elements.
4. Bane of Doom or Bane of Agony with Amplify Curse.
5. Immolate.
6. Corruption.
7. Conflagrate.
8. Shadowburn (Shadow and Flame removes its limits).
9. Incinerate as the filler.
10. Life Tap.

**Cleave:** Bane of Havoc.
