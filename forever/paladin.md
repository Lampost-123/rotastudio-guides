# Paladin — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Paladin guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/holy-paladin/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/protection-paladin/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/retribution-paladin/
- https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/paladin-leveling-guide

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

## Holy

**Priority:**
1. Keep an aura up.
2. Keep a Blessing up (Blessing of Kings by default).
3. Oath of Light on the tank.
4. Keep the Judgement debuff up using Seal of Light or Seal of Wisdom (Sacred Arbiter refreshes it).
5. Divine Favor, then Holy Shock or Holy Light.
6. Group damage: Light's Vigil, then Holy Shock twice. Place Light's Vigil on each party beforehand.
7. Holy Light on Infusion of Light procs.
8. Holy Shock.
9. Flash of Light or Holy Light.

**Mana:** mana fully regenerates after 5 seconds without spending any.

## Protection

**Priority:**
1. Keep Righteous Fury up.
2. Aura: Devotion or Retribution.
3. Blessing of Kings.
4. Seal of Fury (Seal of Righteousness when you aren't tanking).
5. Holy Shield.
6. Templar's Bulwark for emergencies.
7. Swift Judgement, then Judgement, as an emergency taunt or threat burst.
8. Holy Strike.
9. Judgement (it taunts while Seal of Fury is active).
10. Consecration.
11. Exorcism on Undead and Demons.

## Retribution

**Priority:**
1. Keep an aura up.
2. Blessing of Kings.
3. Keep the Seal of the Crusader Judgement debuff up (Sacred Arbiter).
4. Holy Strike.
5. Judgement.
6. Hammer of Wrath below 20% health.
7. Twist Seal of Command and Seal of Righteousness through Unsealed Wrath: the seal echoes onto the next auto attack, so swap seals each swing.
8. Consecration.
9. Exorcism.

**Notes:**
- Before level 40: use Seal of Command with a weapon speed of 3.0 or slower, otherwise Seal of Righteousness.
- Mana: cast Judgement and Holy Strike back to back.
- Champion of the Light turns Intellect into damage.
