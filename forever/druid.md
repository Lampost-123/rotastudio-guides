# Druid — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Druid guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them. No leveling guide was found for Druid.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/balance-druid/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/feral-druid/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/restoration-druid/

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

## Balance

**Priority:**
1. Moonkin Form.
2. Keep Moonfire and Insect Swarm up.
3. Starfire with Eclipse stacks.
4. Wrath.

## Feral (Cat)

**Priority:**
1. Faerie Fire (Feral).
2. Shred on Omen of Clarity procs.
3. Shifting Power below 60 energy (powershifting).
4. Rip at 5 combo points.
5. Rake.
6. Shred.
7. Faerie Fire.
8. Ferocious Bite when it won't delay Rip.

## Feral (Bear)

**Priority:**
1. Dire Bear Form.
2. Frenzied Regeneration for emergencies.
3. Keep Lacerate at 5 stacks.
4. Faerie Fire (Feral).
5. Demoralizing Roar.
6. Maul with excess rage.
7. Primal Bite.
8. Lacerate while below 5 stacks.
9. Swipe.

## Restoration

**Priority:**
1. Nature's Swiftness, then Healing Touch.
2. Tranquility.
3. Wild Growth.
4. Swiftmend.
5. Blanket the raid with Rejuvenation before damage (Gift of the Earthmother, Nature's Splendor).
6. Rejuvenation and Regrowth on tanks.
7. Regrowth or Healing Touch.
