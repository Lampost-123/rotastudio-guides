# Warrior — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Warrior guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/arms-warrior/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/fury-warrior/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/protection-warrior/
- https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/warrior-leveling-guide

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

## Arms

**Priority:**
1. Be in Battle Stance.
2. Keep Battle Shout up.
3. Recklessness with on-use effects, ideally during execute.
4. Bloodrage and Reckless Swings for rage.
5. Sunder Armor while the target has fewer than 5 stacks.
6. Heroic Strike with excess rage.
7. Overpower on Bloodthrill procs or after a dodge.
8. Mortal Strike.
9. Rend.
10. Execute below 20% health.
11. Slam (it doesn't reset the swing timer).
12. Spearing Strike.
13. Hamstring.

## Fury

**Priority:**
1. Be in Berserker Stance. Swap to Battle Stance for Rend, Overpower and Spearing Strike.
2. Keep Battle Shout up.
3. Recklessness and Death Wish during execute.
4. Bloodrage and Reckless Swings for rage.
5. Sunder Armor.
6. Spearing Strike, only against Dragonkin and Giants.
7. Execute.
8. Heroic Strike or Hamstring with excess rage.
9. Bloodthirst.
10. Whirlwind.
11. Overpower after a dodge.
12. Rend.

**Build note:** takes Sweeping Strikes.

## Protection

**Priority:**
1. Be in Defensive Stance.
2. Keep Battle Shout up.
3. Shield Wall and Last Stand for emergencies.
4. Bloodrage and Reckless Swings for rage.
5. Keep Sunder Armor at 5 stacks.
6. Thunder Clap.
7. Demoralizing Shout.
8. Shield Block against physical damage.
9. Heroic Strike with excess rage.
10. Shield Slam.
11. Revenge.
12. Sunder Armor.

**Notes:** one-hand weapon and shield. Charge and Thunder Clap can be used in Defensive Stance.

## Leveling

Source: Maxroll's Warrior leveling guide.

- **Rotation:** Charge in, Battle Shout, Rend, Overpower when it lights up, Sunder Armor while above about 70% health, Heroic Strike to dump rage, Cleave on 2+ targets. Add Mortal Strike and Whirlwind, or Bloodthirst, once you have them.
- **Key talents:** Improved Tactical Mastery, Anger Management, Sweeping Strikes, Bloodthrill, Death Wish, Gore Drinker.
