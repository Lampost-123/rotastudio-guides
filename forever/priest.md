# Priest — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Priest guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/discipline-priest/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/holy-priest/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/shadow-priest/
- https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/priest-leveling-guide

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

## Discipline (healing)

**Priority:**
1. Power Infusion on a caster.
2. Inner Focus before Prayer of Healing.
3. Spread Power Word: Shield before raid damage.
4. Power Word: Shield on the tank (Renewed Hope).
5. Keep Inspiration on the tank.
6. Penance.
7. Flash Heal or Binding Heal.
8. Prayer of Healing.

## Holy

**Priority:**
1. Alternate between different heals (Litany of Light rewards it).
2. Inner Focus with Greater Heal or Prayer of Healing.
3. Renew.
4. Inspiration.
5. Prayer of Mending.
6. Prayer of Healing.
7. Flash Heal or Binding Heal.
8. Greater Heal.
9. Heal.

## Shadow

**Priority:**
1. Stay in Shadowform (spells cost 50% less mana).
2. Vampiric Embrace during group damage.
3. Shadow Word: Pain.
4. Devouring Plague.
5. Mind Blast.
6. Shadow Word: Death.
7. Mind Flay (with Inner Focus).
8. Shadow Word: Death while moving.

**Key talents:** Devouring Contagion (cleave and spread), Early Demise (execute).

## Leveling

Source: Maxroll's Priest leveling guide.

- **Spec:** Discipline to level 39 (Wand Specialization, Spirit Tap, Penance), then Shadow from 40.
- **Damage:** wands do most of the damage while leveling.
