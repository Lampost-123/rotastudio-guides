# Shaman — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Shaman guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/elemental-shaman/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/enhancement-shaman/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/restoration-shaman/
- https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/shaman-leveling-guide

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

**All specs:** Call of the Elements drops all four totems at once, Totemic Projection moves them, and Ghost Wolf is instant.

## Elemental

**Priority:**
1. Searing, Mana Spring, Earth and Air totems through Call of the Elements.
2. Elemental Mastery with on-use effects. Spam Chain Lightning while it's active.
3. Flame Shock.
4. Chain Lightning.
5. Lightning Bolt.
6. While moving: Flame Shock or Earth Shock.

## Enhancement

**Priority:**
1. Flametongue Weapon.
2. Strength of Earth, Mana Spring, Searing, and Windfury or Grace of Air totems.
3. Rage of the Farseer (the Shaman's own Bloodlust) with on-use effects.
4. Lightning Bolt at 5 Maelstrom Weapon stacks.
5. Stormstrike.
6. Flame Shock, or Earth Shock if Flame Shock is already on the target.
7. Fire Nova.

## Restoration

**Priority:**
1. Totems.
2. Water Shield.
3. Ancestral Healing on the tank.
4. Mana Tide Totem.
5. Nature's Swiftness, then Healing Wave or Chain Heal.
6. Riptide on the tank and on damaged players.
7. Chain Heal, starting on Riptide targets.
8. Healing Wave or Lesser Healing Wave.

## Leveling

Source: Maxroll's Shaman leveling guide.

- **Spec:** Enhancement (Mental Dexterity, Stormstrike, Improved Stormstrike).
- **Weapon imbue:** Rockbiter Weapon until Windfury Weapon at level 30.
