# Mage — WoW Forever

Condensed from the public WoW Forever Mage guides (compiled 2026-11-08). Priorities are in the order the sources give them.

Sources:
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/arcane-mage/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/fire-mage/
- https://wowtbc.gg/warcraftforever/class-guides/frost-mage/
- https://maxroll.gg/wow-forever/class-guides/mage-leveling-guide

Caveat: the wowtbc.gg talent grids give point counts only, with no talent names. How downranking behaves is unknown, so one rank of each spell is listed.

## Arcane

**Priority:**
1. Mage Armor.
2. Arcane Power with on-use effects.
3. Pyroblast at 3 Heating Up stacks.
4. Fire Blast.
5. Arcane Missiles on Missile Barrage.
6. Arcane Wrath (with Presence of Mind).
7. Arcane Blast while below 4 stacks (tune this to your mana and gear).
8. Scorch.
9. While moving: Fire Blast or Arcane Explosion.

## Fire

**Priority:**
1. Mage Armor.
2. Keep Improved Scorch at 5 stacks.
3. Pyroblast at 3 Heating Up stacks.
4. Fire Blast.
5. Frostfire Bolt (or Fireball, depending on the build).
6. While moving: Fire Blast or Arcane Explosion.

**AoE:** Flamestrike, Blast Wave, Arcane Explosion.

## Frost

**Priority:**
1. Mage Armor.
2. Ice Barrier.
3. Cone of Cold at 5 Winter's Chill stacks (Cold Snap resets it).
4. Ice Lance on Fingers of Frost.
5. Frostbolt.
6. While moving: Fire Blast or Arcane Explosion.

## Leveling

Source: Maxroll's Mage leveling guide.

- Use a wand to save mana.
- Evocation comes at level 20 and Mage Armor at 34.
