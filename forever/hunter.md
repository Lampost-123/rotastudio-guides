# Hunter — WoW Forever

Condensed from four WoW Forever Hunter guides (compiled 2026-11-08). The three wowtbc.gg spec pages are short and mostly give rotation lists, so the sections below are thin where the sources are silent.

Sources:

Caveat: the talent tables on the wowtbc.gg pages list only point counts per row, with no talent names. Named talents below come from the prose or the Maxroll guide.

## Beast Mastery

**Build summary:** The wowtbc.gg page names no talents beyond the new Summon Hawk spell, and notes that Intimidation now has a damage component. Maxroll's key leveling talents: Deadly Aspects, Pathfinding (moved in a Nov 4 tree update), Improved Revive Pet, Bestial Swiftness, Rapid Killing, Summon Hawk, and 1 point in Improved Mend Pet.

**Single-target priority (wowtbc.gg order):**
1. Use a Cat or Raptor family pet.
2. Use Aspect of the Hawk.
3. Use Trueshot Aura.
4. Maintain Hunter's Mark.
5. Cast Rapid Fire, Bestial Wrath, and any on-use effects.
6. Cast Intimidation.
7. When melee weaving, always pair Raptor Strike with Immolation Trap or Wing Clip.
8. Maintain Serpent Sting.
9. Cast Summon Hawk if fewer than two hawks are active. Otherwise cast Arcane Shot.
10. Cast Multi-Shot.

Auto Shot and weave timing are not covered beyond step 7.

**AoE:** Multi-Shot is last in the priority. No separate AoE section.

**Cooldowns:** Rapid Fire, Bestial Wrath, and on-use trinkets or items.

**Aspects, stings, marks:** Aspect of the Hawk, Trueshot Aura, Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting.

**Pet notes:** Use a Cat or Raptor family pet. Maxroll keeps the pet on passive with /petattack, /petfollow, /petstay and /petattack [@mouseover,harm,nodead][] macros, and uses Growl manually.

**Mana notes:** Mana regenerates fully only after 5 seconds without casting. Let the pet and Auto Shot finish mobs below about 30% health when safe.

**Notes:** Maxroll warns Summon Hawk is buggy at launch and currently recommends mostly Arcane Shot (see Leveling).

## Marksmanship

**Build summary:** MM gains Sniper Shot as a new level 30 talent. The page also mentions Rapid Recuperation for mana and Lone Wolf for playing without a pet.

**Single-target priority (wowtbc.gg order):**
1. Use Aspect of the Hawk.
2. Use Trueshot Aura.
3. Maintain Hunter's Mark.
4. Cast Rapid Fire with any on-use effects.
5. When melee weaving, pair Raptor Strike with Immolation Trap or Wing Clip.
6. Maintain Serpent Sting.
7. Cast Sniper Shot.
8. Cast Arcane Shot.
9. Cast Multi-Shot.

**AoE:** Multi-Shot is last on the list. No separate AoE section.

**Cooldowns:** Rapid Fire with on-use effects.

**Aspects, stings, marks:** Aspect of the Hawk, Trueshot Aura, Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting.

**Pet / mana:** Lone Wolf is an option to play without a pet. Rapid Recuperation helps mana.

## Survival

**Build summary:** Survival has a full talent overhaul supporting a melee playstyle or a stronger melee weave, built around Mongoose Bite procs and traps (build labels: 2H Weave and DW Melee). Strider Kick is the new spell. Maxroll: not practical for leveling until about level 40, since the key talents (Strider Kick, Expose Prey, Resourcefulness, Lacerating Strikes) sit at the end of the tree.

**Single-target priority (wowtbc.gg order):**
1. Use Aspect of the Hawk.
2. Maintain Hunter's Mark.
3. Cast Rapid Fire with any on-use effects.
4. When melee weaving, pair Raptor Strike with one of Strider Kick, Mongoose Bite, Immolation Trap, or Wing Clip.
5. Maintain Serpent Sting.
6. Cast Arcane Shot.
7. Cast Multi-Shot.

Mongoose Bite is used when it procs.

**AoE:** Multi-Shot is last on the list.

**Cooldowns:** Rapid Fire with on-use effects.

**Aspects, stings, marks:** Aspect of the Hawk, Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting.

## Leveling

Source: Maxroll's Hunter leveling guide (updated November 4–5, 2026).

- **Spec:** Beast Mastery is recommended. Survival is not recommended until about level 40.
- **Questing rotation:** Hunter's Mark while closing distance and send the pet in. Aimed Shot as the pet runs in, or Multi-Shot if mobs are nearby. Summon Hawk when you lack two hawks and aren't low on mana, otherwise Arcane Shot. Multi-Shot on 2+ nearby enemies. Raptor Strike and Wing Clip enemies that reach melee, then Auto Shot to finish.
- **Arcane Shot vs Summon Hawk:** Arcane Shot was buffed in late hotfixes and Hawk AI is buggy, so Maxroll currently recommends mostly Arcane Shot.
- **Dungeon rotation:** Hunter's Mark and Arcane Shot while running in. Immolation Trap. Mongoose Bite whenever it procs. Strider Kick and Raptor Strike on cooldown. On 2+ targets use Explosive Trap instead of Immolation Trap and step out to Multi-Shot on cooldown.
- **Serpent Sting:** used roughly from level 4 to 10, then dropped because mobs die too fast.
- **Pet:** Tame Beast at 10. Feed Pet for happiness, Beast Training for training points, re-tame beasts to learn higher Claw and Bite ranks.
- **Swing timer:** enable it under Options > Advanced options > Enable Swing Timer.
- **Stats:** Spirit, Agility, Strength while leveling. Highest-DPS ranged and main-hand weapons.
- **Common mistake:** running out of mana from casting constantly. Let auto attacks finish mobs below about 30%.
